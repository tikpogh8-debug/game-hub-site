Delete your Game Hub account and data

Game Hub is made by Playground Games. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

In the game (the quickest way)

  1. Open Game Hub and tap the gear at the top right (Settings), then Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete my account to confirm.

The account is erased at once.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address, and the sign-in with Google if you used it.
  • Your results saved to the account (levels, stars, best scores).
  • Your public leaderboard name, picture and country, and your scores on the leaderboards.

Results stored only on your phone are not touched. Uninstalling the game removes them. Deleting the account does not delete your Google account.

If you cannot open the game

Write to tikpogh8@gmail.com from the email address of your account and say you want your account deleted. We delete it within 30 days, and normally much sooner. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

Questions
