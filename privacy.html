Privacy policy: Game Hub

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who is responsible: Playground Games, contact: tikpogh8@gmail.com

This policy says what the game Game Hub (Bloomlight, Skyline Stack and Unscrew Town) stores, what it sends, and how you can delete it.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your results (levels, stars, best scores, which tips you have seen) and your settings (sound, vibration, language, reduced motion).
  • This stays on the phone unless you create an account.

What is sent to our server, and only if you have an account

The server is a Supabase project (supabase.com), a hosted database. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.

  • Account: your email address and a password (the password is stored only as a salted hash by Supabase; we cannot read it). Guest players get an anonymous account with no email.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells the game your Google email address and a Google account ID. We use them only to create or find your account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your results: the same results as above (not your settings), so they follow you to another phone.
  • Leaderboard and profile: the public name you choose, a picture you pick from the game's built-in avatars, an optional country you pick yourself (we do not read your location), and your best score and rank per game. Other players can see these on the leaderboard and when they tap your name. Pick a name that does not identify you; you can change the picture and remove the country any time (Account > Edit profile).
  • Technical logs kept by the hosting provider (such as IP address and time of requests).

We do not sell your data, show you personalised ads, or track you across other apps or websites. The game has no analytics today.

Why

To save and sync your progress, to show the leaderboard, and to keep the service secure (for example, to stop forged scores).

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Results on your phone stay on your phone until you uninstall the game.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings (or Account) > Account > Delete account. This permanently erases your account, your cloud results, your leaderboard name, picture, country and your scores from the server. Results stored on your phone are not touched; uninstalling the game removes them. If you cannot use the app, write to tikpogh8@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete it. The page https://tikpogh8-debug.github.io/game-hub-site/delete-account.html explains the same steps.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California), you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Use the delete button above or write to tikpogh8@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for important changes, the game tells you.
